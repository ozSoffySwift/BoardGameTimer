# Privacy Policy — Meeple Clock

**Effective date:** July 6, 2026

Meeple Clock ("the app") is a board game turn timer developed by Oz Soffy. This
policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: **the app
does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

## Data Collection

Meeple Clock does **not** collect any data. Specifically, the app has:

- **No accounts** — you never sign up, sign in, or provide an email address.
- **No analytics** — the app contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- **No ads** — the app displays no advertising of any kind.
- **No network access for your data** — nothing you enter in the app is ever
  transmitted off your device.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following information **only on your device**, using Apple's
standard on-device storage:

- Player names and colors you enter when setting up a game
- Game history (game name, date, rounds played, and each player's timed totals)
- Your app settings (such as default turn limit, sound, and haptics preferences)

This information never leaves your device unless **you** choose to share it —
for example, by using the share button on a results screen, which uses the
standard iOS share sheet under your control. Deleting the app deletes all of
this data. You can also clear your game history at any time from the app's
Settings screen.

## Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app ever changes these practices, this policy will
be updated here before that version is released, and the change will be noted
in the app's App Store release notes.
