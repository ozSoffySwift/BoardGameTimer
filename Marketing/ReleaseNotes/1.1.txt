Countdown mode is here.

When you set up a game you now pick how the clocks run:

STOPWATCH — exactly as before. Every player's clock counts up, no limit.

COUNTDOWN — each player gets a fixed time bank, 30 minutes by default, that
counts down as they think. Run out and your clock keeps going in red rather
than cutting you off, so the table decides what running out means for your
game. The per-turn limit still works alongside it.

Also in this update:

• The player meeples have been redrawn from scratch as proper Carcassonne-style
  pawns, replacing the old blocky figures.
• Results for countdown games show how much time each player had left.

Your saved game history carries over untouched.
